# Educational decision record

> **Status: proposal.** The candidate *constitutive artefact* of EI ([uniqueness argument](../discipline/uniqueness-argument.md)). Not yet piloted.

## The seven core fields

| Field | Content | Theory it answers to |
|---|---|---|
| **Aim** | Target competence and educational purpose; who set it | Purpose before effectiveness [@Biesta2007Works]; "cognition" in the assessment triangle [@NRC2001Knowing] |
| **Decision** | Actor, level, options really available (including doing nothing) | Room to manoeuvre; prioritisation [@Becher2020Teaching] |
| **Evidence** | Observations used, with source, legal basis and quality | "Observation" [@NRC2001Knowing]; stewardship [@Wieczorek2026Beyond] |
| **Inference** | What was concluded, and the warrant | "Interpretation" [@NRC2001Knowing]; evidence-centred design [@Mislevy2003Structure]; argument-based validity [@Kane2013Validating] |
| **Action** | What was actually done, including departures from the plan | Implementation fidelity [@NCII2013Databased]; disciplined improvisation [@Becher2020Teaching] |
| **Response** | What followed, observed by whom, after what lag | Progress monitoring [@NCII2013Databased] |
| **Expiry** | When the inference must be re-established or deleted | Measurement alters conduct [@Hautz2026Algorithmic]; retention and data stewardship |

## Three levels, one structure

- **Classroom:** which support to give a learner or a group next.
- **Institution:** which platform to adopt, which data to retain.
- **System:** a procurement rule, a data-protection impact assessment, an AI approval.

Using the same structure at all three levels links learner data to the anchor paper's governance object [@Tegelbeckers2026Education]. It also makes a new question answerable: *which institutional decisions constrain which classroom decisions?*

## Relation to existing standards

xAPI and Caliper record *what happened* [@ADLxAPISpec; @IMSCaliper]. The decision record adds *why, with what warrant, among which options, and until when*. It should map onto these standards, not compete with them. Learning-design work already documents pedagogical intent for interpreting analytics [@Lockyer2013Informing]. That is the closest neighbour and must be cited.

## Design commitments

- Learners are decision-makers in their own records, as appropriate for their age. A learner-held view is computed from the records ([lifelong learner model](lifelong-learner-model.md)).
- Records are never used to evaluate individual teachers. This is written into every partnership.
- Capture cost is a **tested hypothesis**, not an assumption. Instructors' real responses include waiting and not acting [@Wise2019Teaching].
- The model must work without any prohibited inference ([AI Act](../governance/ai-act-and-learner-data.md)).

## Risks

It can sound administrative. Teachers may read it as surveillance of their own work. It may privilege the institution's view over the learner's. Measurement specialists may call it repackaged validity theory. Each risk has a matching commitment above.
