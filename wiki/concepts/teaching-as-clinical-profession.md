# Teaching as a clinical profession

## Core source

Becher and Lefstein examine the clinical model of teaching: what diagnosis, treatment and inference require in teaching, how teachers tend to perform them, and how the context of teachers' work shapes them [@Becher2020Teaching]. Fundamental differences from medicine limit a direct transfer, so they **adapt** the three clinical acts:

| Medicine | Adapted for teaching | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Diagnosis | **Prioritisation and noticing** | With many learners and goals at once, the skill is deciding what to attend to |
| Treatment | **Disciplined improvisation** | Plans are adapted in the moment, within principled limits |
| Inference | **Room to manoeuvre** | Judgement about what is possible in a given context, not only what is optimal |

## Cautions the authors raise

- A deficit view: diagnosis directs attention to what learners lack.
- Narrow goals: "what works?" hides "for what purpose, for whom?" (compare [@Biesta2007Works]).
- Neglect of the collective: a teacher acts on an interacting group, not a series of cases.
- Over-reliance on measurement displacing equity and broader aims.

## Implications for EI data (own synthesis)

1. The unit is the **decision under constraint**, not the learner's state alone. This feeds the decision and action fields of the [decision record](educational-decision-record.md).
2. Data must capture **what was prioritised and set aside**, and **how action departed from plan**. Activity logs cannot.
3. **Context and resources** are part of the explanation and the response, not background. Change the task, the scaffolding or the environment before attributing difficulty to the learner (compare DBI's environmental adaptations [@NCII2013Databased]).

## Related evidence from medicine

AI capability did not translate into physicians' diagnostic competence in a randomised trial [@Goh2024Large]. The parallel in education is that tool design, not access, decided the direction of effects [@Bastani2025Generative]. Medical education is now proposing dedicated Educational Informatics teams [@Turner2024Takes]. See [medicine-analogy propositions](../propositions/medicine-analogy.md).
