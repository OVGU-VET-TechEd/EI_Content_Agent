# Education Informatics knowledge wiki

This wiki is the structured, growing knowledge of **Education Informatics (EI)** as a discipline under construction. EI_Content_Agent writes and maintains it, and every page is citable back to a reference in the private literature database (`[@citekey]`).

## Reading order

1. [Definition, object and terms](discipline/definition-and-object.md): what EI is proposed to be.
2. [Six criteria of disciplinarity](discipline/disciplinarity-criteria.md): the standard it is judged against, and its current status.
3. [Uniqueness argument](discipline/uniqueness-argument.md): the answer to "a new field has no method of its own".
4. [Rival fields](discipline/rival-fields.md): who could already claim EI's object or method.
5. [Open questions](discipline/open-questions.md): decisions, research agenda, weak claims.
