# Open questions and research agenda

## Open decisions

| Decision | Options | Current recommendation | What would settle it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Core unit of the field | (A) [lifelong learner model](../concepts/lifelong-learner-model.md) vs (B) [educational decision record](../concepts/educational-decision-record.md) | **B, with A as a learner-held view computed from B.** Stronger originality claim, survives the AI Act, links to governance | A capture pilot (e.g. ten educators each record five real decisions in the seven fields) plus a read of both one-page versions by a sympathetic and a sceptical educational scientist |
| Name of the method | "Decision-anchored evidence engineering" is a working label | Keep as a label until the method is published | Uptake; a short methods paper |
| Scope of biometrics | None, gated adult lab study, or full | Gated adult lab study only; never in schools ([AI Act](../governance/ai-act-and-learner-data.md)) | Legal opinion and a decision-value result |

## Research priorities

From the anchor paper's agenda [@Tegelbeckers2026Education]:

- Empirical study of the roles that already perform EI functions in ministries and school systems without disciplinary recognition.
- Comparative analysis of national digital-education governance models.
- A systematic synthesis of comparative digital-inequality evidence in education.
- Validation studies for the competency profile.
- Evaluation frameworks for AI and platform procurement in public education.
- Case studies of locally governed model deployment.

Added by the [uniqueness argument](uniqueness-argument.md):

- **H1 Representability.** Does a small core model represent real decisions across sectors and levels at acceptable capture cost?
- **H2 Decision value.** Do work samples and instructional history carry most decision-relevant value? Under which pre-registered conditions does added context or sensor data change defensible decisions?
- **H3 Expiry.** Does the validity of inferences decay measurably, and differently by construct type?
- **H4 Transportability.** Do decision rules transport better when aims and conditions are recorded than when only activity data are shared?
- **Calibration.** Can the infrastructure reproduce the learning-styles null and expertise reversal? ([@Pashler2008Learning; @Tetzlaff2025Cornerstone])

## Claims resting on weak evidence

| Claim | Basis | How to firm it up |
|---|---|---|
| No standard data model for educational decisions exists | A few searches plus adjacent reading | Systematic, double-coded rival audit |
| No micro-randomised trials in education | One search that returned only platforms | Dedicated search; some probably exist |
| Interface fields are defined by a constitutive artefact plus a domain validity test | Own synthesis of a few sources | Literature check; short paper |
| The combination of education's features is specific to education | Own argument | Compare with nursing, social work and other relational professions |
| Decision capture is practicable | No data | Capture pilot |
| Semantic construct mapping transfers to educational constructs | Shown for personality items only [@Wulff2025Semantic] | Pilot on a small set of educational measures |
