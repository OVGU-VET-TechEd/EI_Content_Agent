# Rival and neighbouring fields

Two lists. **Neighbouring labels** use a similar name. **Rival fields** could claim EI's object or method combination. Residual risk is the risk that the rival already does what EI claims (low / medium / **high**).

## Neighbouring labels

| Label | What it means there | Relation to EI as defined here |
|---|---|---|
| Education Informatics (TCR, 2010) | Subdiscipline about discovering, organising and communicating educational information [@Collins2010Proposal; @Carr2010Policy] | Precursor; EI extends it to governance, architecture and AI. The venue did not consolidate [@Carr2010Introduction] |
| Eduinformatics / IR philosophy | Education plus informatics for analysing student and institutional data, analogised to bioinformatics [@Takamatsu2024Proposing] | Object is institutional research; no governance or procurement responsibility |
| eLearning Informatics | Informatics within an eLearning framework, from automation to intelligent solutions [@Ivanova2020Elearning] | Technology-centred object |
| "Informatics in education" (descriptive) | Digital tools that make learning flexible and inclusive [@Rooroh2024Implementation] | The descriptive usage the anchor paper separates EI from [@Tegelbeckers2026Education] |
| Educational Informatics teams (medical education) | Institutional teams with authority, funding and assessment frameworks for AI in medical education [@Turner2024Takes] | Closest *professional* precedent; supports the professional pathway |

## Rival audit

| Rival | What it does | What it lacks for the EI claim | Residual risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Learning Analytics / EDM | Traces of learner activity, prediction, explanatory learner models [@Romero2020Educational; @Rose2019Explanatory] | Unit is learner activity; governance and architecture marginal (anchor paper, Table 2); theory grounding described as weak [@Gasevic2014Forget] | Medium |
| Educational Data Science | Convergence of LA and EDM; data-science methods in education research [@Cerezo2024Reviewing; @McFarland2021Education] | Methodological umbrella; no decision model, no governance responsibility | Medium |
| Learning design + LA | Documented pedagogical intent used to interpret analytics [@Lockyer2013Informing] | Intent at course level, not decisions across levels; no expiry or contestability | Medium (closest neighbour for "intent as data") |
| xAPI / IMS Caliper | Standard statements about learning activity with context [@ADLxAPISpec; @IMSCaliper] | Describe what happened, not the aim, the options, the warrant or the expiry | Low (build on them, do not compete) |
| Evidence-centred design / argument-based validity | Explicit inference chains in assessment [@Mislevy2003Structure; @Kane2013Validating] | Centred on assessment arguments, not classroom, institutional and procurement decisions and their consequences | Medium ("this is just validity theory") |
| Data-based decision making / DBI | Goal, evidence, action and evaluation cycle [@Schildkamp2019Data; @NCII2013Databased; @Mandinach2012Perfect] | Practice frameworks without a shared computable model; DBI targets intensive support; IES evidence rated low [@Hamilton2009Using] | Medium (cite as origin, not as enemy) |
| **Learning Engineering** | Learning sciences applied through human-centred engineering design and data-informed iteration [@Goodell2022Learning] | Defined as process and practice, not by a formal decision model or governance responsibility | **High: strongest rival.** Check whether it claims a shared data model |
| Open / lifelong learner models | Learner-owned long-term models [@Kay2008Lifelong] | Unit is the learner; decisions and aims are not modelled | High if EI's core unit is the learner model; low if it is the decision record ([lifelong learner model](../concepts/lifelong-learner-model.md)) |
| Precision education | Data on individual differences to personalise education [@Hart2016Precision; @Triola2023Precision] | Personalisation logic; no governance object; deficit and surveillance risks | Medium |
| Experimentation platforms and MRTs | Randomisation inside software; time-varying effects [@Klasnja2015Microrandomized] | Software-embedded; does not model teacher, institutional or system decisions | Medium (education MRTs may exist; search not done) |
| IS design science | Design-oriented method [@Osterle2011Memorandum; @Helfert2008Business] | Coded as largely silent on pedagogy and disclaiming governance (anchor paper) | Low (it is the parent method) |
| German Bildungsinformatik groups (e.g. DIPF) | Indicator repositories, educational technologies | Not yet audited in this knowledge base | Unknown: audit |

## What the searches do and do not show

No standard data model for teacher, institutional and system decisions has been found, but this rests on a few searches plus adjacent reading, not a systematic review. **A failed search is not evidence of absence.** Before a proposal relies on this gap, run a documented search, ideally double-coded to address the anchor review's single-coder limitation. A positive finding would change the strategy: extend the existing model, and place originality in cross-level validation.

## Precedents for discipline formation

Learning Analytics narrated its own emergence as a discipline [@Siemens2013Learning]. Data Science Education did the same more recently [@Mike2023Birth]. The Learning Sciences tested coherence through programme analysis [@Sommerhoff2018Teach]. These are models for EI's own criterion 6 work; they are not evidence that EI exists.
